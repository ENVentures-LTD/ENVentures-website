The company

What guides the work

Know who it's for

I want to be clear about who an app is for and what it will help them do, before deciding what goes into it.

Stay responsible for it

The work continues after release. ENVentures looks after the app, the services behind it and its website, so maintenance and support stay part of the job.

Make the next version more useful

As an app develops, I want to keep asking whether a change helps the person using it. That applies to a small fix as much as a new feature.
