Fameally

Helping households bring meal ideas, weekly plans and the food shop together.

Visit FameallyDownload Fameally

Why this product

Less work between the meal idea and the shop.

A useful recipe can end up separate from the weekly plan, while ingredients need copying onto another list. In a shared household, changes also need to reach the person doing the shopping.

ENVentures LTD develops Fameally to connect those everyday tasks. The app gives individuals, couples and families a place to save meals, organise the week and build shopping lists from their plans.

Fameally is the consumer brand. ENVentures is the company developing and operating the product.

The product workflow

Save → Plan → Shop

Save meals and recipes

Keep meal ideas in one place, ready to return to when choosing what to cook.

Plan the week

Organise saved meals into a weekly plan that fits the household’s schedule.

Build the shopping list

Generate a shopping list from planned meals, then check what is already at home and add other essentials.

Built for everyday use

A shared view of the week.

Household sharing lets people use shared meals, plans and shopping lists, so the weekly routine does not have to sit with one person.

Fameally is available for iPhone, iPad and Android. Current features, household access, plans and download links are explained on the Fameally website.
