From the founder

It started with a shopping list.

I started ENVentures while trying to make a shopping list and meal planning app that actually worked for me and my family. Along the way, I realised it could be useful to other people too.

I want to take the same approach with future apps. Whatever I build next, it should have a clear benefit for the person using it.
